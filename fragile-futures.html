Series

Fragile Futures

Fragile Futures reflects on movement, resilience, and coexistence through wildlife, flora, organic and inorganic forms. Nature is treated not as a distant landscape, but as an intimate invitation to be in the present.

Mediums vary (oil, acrylic, watercolor, gouache) as materials are guided by the concept.

Growth I Gouache on paper · Singapore · 2026 Materials lead the process — growth as a rhythm.
Growth II Gouache on paper · Singapore · 2026 Our cages may be golden but they are to grow out of.
Outgrow Oil on canvas · Singapore · 2026 "Outgrow" is an ode to resilience in the face of restriction and limit, an inspiration to keep growing out of any imposed barrier - or cage, even a golden one.
Earthly Crops Watercolor and ink on paper · Singapore · 2026 A tribute to natural beauty — persistent and quiet.
Rays Underwater Mixed media on watercolour paper · Singapore · 2026 Rays of light pierce through the water depths, revealing what is hidden to the human eye - the beauty of the sea inhabitants and underwater textures. Gliding through the seemingly still waters are the majestic manta rays, royalty of the sea. More modest citizens can be seen hiding in the corals.
