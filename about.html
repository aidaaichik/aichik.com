Artist statement

"To appreciate life, in all its shape and form, is the ultimate human purpose, and the highest level of human intelligence." - A.Aichik

My practice centers on imaginative, creature‑led paintings that function as allegories — visual fables where fantasy becomes a language for emotional truth.

I work across watercolor, ink, oil, pastels, as well as fabric and embroidery. Watercolor introduces translucency and chance, ink provides structure and contrast, and oil allows me to build depth gradually from canvas to the surface.

My works are open narratives. The creatures act as anchors for interpretation, inviting viewers to project their own memories, fears, and hopes into the myth.

Bio

Aida Aichik is an internationally shaped visual artist based in Singapore. Her practice is rooted in a deep dialogue with wildlife and the natural world, exploring themes of movement, resilience, and coexistence.

Formally trained in Moscow as a child and shaped by years living and working across Russia, the UK, Germany, Malaysia, and Singapore, her work blends disciplined observation with a contemporary, nomadic sensibility.
